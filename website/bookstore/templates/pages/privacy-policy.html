1. Introduction

Nageen Prakashan mein hum aapki privacy ki dil se izzat karte hain. Yeh policy batati hai ki jab aap humari website ya mobile app ka upyog karte hain, toh hum aapki jaankari kaise collect aur safe rakhte hain. Humari koshish hai ki aapka data hamesha surakshit rahe.

2. Data We Collect

Hum niche di gayi jaankari collect kar sakte hain:

  • Aapka naam aur sampark ki jaankari (Email, Phone number).
  • Address (Order delivery ke liye).
  • Payment details (Securely processed via third-party gateways).
  • Usage data (Aap app kaise use karte hain).

3. How We Use Data

Hum aapka data sirf aapko behtar service dene ke liye use karte hain, jaise order process karna, naye offers ki jaankari dena, aur app experience ko fast banana. Hum aapka data kisi bhi third-party ko bina wajah nahi bechte.

4. Data Security

Aapka data humare liye priority hai. Hum **SSL encryption** aur secure servers ka upyog karte hain taaki koi bhi unauthorized person aapki jaankari tak na pahunch sake.

5. Cookie Policy

Humari website 'Cookies' ka upyog karti hai taaki hum yaad rakh sakein ki aapne cart mein kya dala hai aur aapki pasand kya hai. Aap chahein toh browser setting se ise disable bhi kar sakte hain.
